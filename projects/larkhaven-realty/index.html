01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Every inquiry needs a next step.

Larkhaven Realty is a fictional real estate brokerage with five agents and an estimated 150 inquiries per month. This project models the operational challenges of a small team managing inquiries across a lead journey.

  • Delayed responses to new property inquiries
  • Duplicate leads from multiple sources
  • Manual lead qualification
  • Unassigned leads without clear ownership
  • Missed follow-ups as inquiries move through the pipeline

This is a fictional business scenario, not a paying client engagement. Inquiry volume and team size are scenario assumptions, not measured results.

02 / THE SOLUTION

One connected lead management process.

I built an automation system that captures property inquiries, qualifies them with Claude AI, and passes the information into GoHighLevel through n8n. The workflow checks for existing contacts before creating or updating CRM records and organizing opportunities.

Round-robin routing gives leads an agent owner. GoHighLevel follow-up workflows support the next conversation, appointment scheduling, and ongoing lead tracking.

Capture & qualify→Organize & assign→Follow up & book
03 / AUTOMATION ARCHITECTURE

From first inquiry to booked appointment.

Select any step to see what happens. The diagram explains the automation architecture; it does not execute a live workflow.

  1. ✉STEP 01Lead Inquiry+

    A prospective buyer sends a property inquiry. This is the starting point for capturing lead information.

  2. ↪STEP 02n8n Webhook+

    The webhook receives the inquiry and starts the connected lead intake workflow.

  3. ✦STEP 03Claude AI Qualification+

    Claude extracts budget, buying timeline, and preferred location, then produces a lead score from 1 to 10 with a short explanation.

  4. ⌕STEP 04Duplicate Check+

    n8n checks existing GoHighLevel contacts through its API before deciding whether to create or update a record.

  5. ▤STEP 05GoHighLevel CRM+

    The contact is created or updated, and opportunities are created and organized in the CRM pipeline.

  6. ⇄STEP 06Agent Assignment+

    Round-robin routing distributes incoming leads among the five agents in the fictional brokerage.

  7. ↻STEP 07Automated Follow-Up+

    GoHighLevel workflows manage follow-up stages and responses, including short-term follow-up and long-term nurture.

  8. ▦STEP 08Appointment Booking+

    Booking information connects back to lead tracking so the next stage remains visible in the CRM.
